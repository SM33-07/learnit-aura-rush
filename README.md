## 🎯 What is AURA RUSH?
**AURA RUSH** is an ultra-fast, viral, mobile-first campus mini-game created for the **LearnIT Stall & Membership Drive**. 

Forget boring aptitude quizzes and academic exams. AURA RUSH tests your reflexes, campus street-smarts, meme IQ, psychological grit, and sheer chaotic confidence through **25 rapid-fire interactive challenges** in under 3 minutes.

---

## 📜 Official Rules of Engagement

1. **The Run Structure**:
   - Each player gets **25 unique challenges** drawn dynamically from a curated **100+ challenge bank**.
   - **Zero repeats** and **strictly unique interaction patterns** in every run.
   - Total run duration: **~2.5 to 3 minutes**.

2. **Starting State**:
   - Every participant begins with a base aura score of **`500 AURA`**.
   - Your score continuously updates in real-time in the persistent top banner (`✦ AURA [Score]`).

3. **Timer & Pacing**:
   - Each challenge has a comfortable **16-second countdown timer** (with 5s for discipline checks) to read the scenarios and make your play.
   - Making clutch choices quickly awards full points.
   - Letting the timer expire fumbles the challenge and triggers a penalty (**`-60 AURA 💀`**).

4. **Points & Penalties**:
   - **Clutch / High-Aura moves**: Gain **`+120`** to **`+250 AURA`** with celebratory confetti sparkles.
   - **NPC / Fumble moves**: Lose **`-40`** to **`-150 AURA`** accompanied by a screen shake and red warning flash.
   - Scores cannot drop below `0 AURA`.

## 🕹️ Interaction Mechanics & Mini-Game Types

Every single challenge features a distinct, tactile interaction mechanic:

| Mechanic | Description | Example Challenge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🎯 Deadline Clutch Needle** | An oscillating gauge needle. Tap to stop it dead inside the green maximum aura zone. | *Stop the needle at max aura before the 11:59 deadline.* |
| **⚡ Reflex Eye Blink** | High-tension waiting game. Tap the exact millisecond the eye snaps shut. | *Don't blink. Tap when the professor looks down.* |
| **⏰ Alarm Matrix Speedrun** | Multiple ringing alarms. Identify and dismiss ONLY the correct 7:45 AM class alarm in seconds. | *Dismiss the 7:45 AM lecture alarm without snoozing late.* |
| **🧘 Silent Discipline Hold** | A 5-second countdown where you must **do absolutely nothing**. Touching the screen costs aura. | *Someone dropped a steel bottle in the silent library. Hold still.* |
| **🔍 Memory Locker** | Three campus essentials flash for 1.6s before one is stolen. Guess the missing item. | *Which snack was swiped from your canteen tray?* |
| **🤝 Sequence Combo Breaker** | Memorize and replicate a multi-emoji secret handshake in exact order. | *Repeat: 🤝 → ✌️ → 🗿* |
| **💰 Canteen Budget Allocator** | Allocate ₹80 across campus food items to maximize calories, quantity, and respect. | *Build lunch with ₹80 without starving.* |
| **🔥 Auction & Wagers** | Risk a portion of your current aura score on high-stakes campus gambles. | *Bid for the only working laptop charger in the library.* |
| **⚖️ Split & Versus Dilemmas** | Two-sided split screen comparisons requiring decisive, unhesitating judgment. | *8 AM Class vs. Surprise Viva.* |
| **📱 WhatsApp Group Panic** | Live chat scenario with professor or campus group. Pick the only socially survivable response. | *Professor sent a meme to the official class group.* |
| **🕵️ NPC & Social Stealth** | Dodge hazards, calculate passage routes, and identify zero-aura behavior. | *Crush and Ex enter the canteen at the same time.* |
